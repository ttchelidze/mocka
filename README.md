# MOCKA – Practice Exams

Free, offline practice exams in an exam-center style screen.

**▶ Open MOCKA: https://ttchelidze.github.io/mocka/**

## How to install

1. Open **https://ttchelidze.github.io/mocka/** in **Chrome** or **Edge** on a computer.
2. Click **Install app** in MOCKA's toolbar, or the install icon at the right of the address bar, then **Install**.
3. MOCKA opens in its own window and appears in your Start menu with its icon. From then on it works offline.
4. New to MOCKA? Click **Try demo exams** or open the **User guide** inside the app.

## How to uninstall

1. In the MOCKA window, click the **⋮** (Chrome) or **…** (Edge) menu at the top right → **Uninstall MOCKA**.
   Or right-click **MOCKA** in the Start menu → **Uninstall**.
2. Leave **"Also clear data"** unticked if you want your exams and results back when you reinstall.
   Ticking it deletes them, so click **Export backup** first if you want to keep a copy.

## Your data

Your exams, images and results are stored only on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded.
Use **Export backup** to keep a copy and **Restore backup** to bring it back, for example on a new computer.
